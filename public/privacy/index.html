Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how [App Name] (“the App”, “we”, “our”), published by Mohammad Hossein Karami at https://mhkarami97.ir, handles information when you use it.

1 Who we are

The App is operated by Mohammad Hossein Karami. Contact: mhkarami97@gmail.com.

2 What the App does

[App Name] is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that works offline. All of your data is stored locally on your device, in your browser's storage (for example IndexedDB or localStorage). We do not run servers that store your application data.

3 Information we collect

  • Data you create in the App: [for example tasks, checklists, notes, financial entries]. This data stays on your device.
  • Optional Google Drive backup: If you choose to enable backup, you sign in with your Google Account. The App requests only the following scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, or any other profile information from your Google Account. We do not collect advertising identifiers.

4 How the Google Drive backup works

  • The App stores a backup file of your App data in the hidden “Application Data” folder (appDataFolder) of your own Google Drive.
  • This folder is private to the App. We do not have access to your other Drive files, and other apps cannot see this folder.
  • The backup file is created, updated, and read only by the App running in your browser, using an access token issued by Google to your device. The data goes directly between your browser and Google. It does not pass through any server operated by us.
  • You can disconnect at any time. You can also delete the backup by removing the App's data in your Google Account settings (Security > Third-party apps with account access, or Google Drive > Settings > Manage apps).

5 How we use information

We use your data only to provide the App's features (storing your content locally, and creating and restoring your backup). We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

6 Google API Services User Data Policy

[App Name]'s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7 Sharing and disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer your data to third parties. Your backup is stored only in your own Google Drive and is subject to Google's own privacy policy.

8 Data retention and deletion

  • Local data remains on your device until you clear your browser data or uninstall the App.
  • Your Google Drive backup remains until you delete it or revoke the App's access.
  • To request help with deletion, contact mhkarami97@gmail.com.

9 Security

Data is transferred over HTTPS. [Optional: Backups are encrypted on your device before upload.] No method of storage is completely secure, and you are responsible for protecting your device and Google Account.

10 Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
